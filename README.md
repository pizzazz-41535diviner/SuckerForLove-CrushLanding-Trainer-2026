## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sucker for Love: Crush Landing**. This trainer assists with Crush Landing's apartment exploration, color-based feeding puzzles, inventory management, timed objectives, and branching story progression. It is designed for players who want to experiment with choices and reach different endings without repeatedly restarting scenes.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore every room, test different feeding orders, preserve limited offerings, manage the timed apartment sequence more comfortably, and experience multiple endings with fewer repeated attempts.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Countdown** — Pauses the active time limit while you search the apartment, inspect objects, and decide how to keep Hheily's flame alive.
- **Unlimited Flame Stability** — Prevents the comet's flame from expiring during feeding and progression sequences.
- **Inventory Protection** — Prevents collected offerings from being consumed or lost, allowing safer experimentation with color-coded feeding choices.

- **No Combat Mode** — Crush Landing does not use a conventional combat system. The trainer focuses on survival pressure, exploration, feeding, puzzles, and story choices instead.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the hand-drawn apartment and nearby areas by moving between available locations and interacting with visible objects. Activate the trainer after entering gameplay, then use the frozen timer to examine rooms, inspect containers, and respond to visitors without rushing.

### Tools & Inventory

Collected objects and offerings are central to progression. Pay attention to each item's color and purpose before feeding it to Hheily. Inventory Protection is useful when testing different combinations, since using the wrong item or exhausting a color can restrict later options.

### World & Environment

The apartment is a navigable 360-degree environment filled with examinable objects, puzzle clues, potential offerings, and story interactions. The trainer can reduce time pressure, but it does not replace required conversations, discoveries, or branching decisions.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no traditional combat in Crush Landing. Threats are handled through exploration, resource management, timed events, feeding decisions, and interactions at the apartment door. Use the trainer's gameplay options to manage those pressures rather than looking for health or ammunition enhancements.
